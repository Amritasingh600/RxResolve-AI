Title: Step Therapy Policy (Sample)

FICTIONAL SAMPLE POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. This document was written for the RxResolveAI student project. It does not describe any real insurance company, plan or medication.

Purpose
Under step therapy, the sample plans cover certain medications only after the patient has tried a preferred first-line medication. A claim without a documented trial of the prerequisite drug is rejected with "Step therapy required" (reject code 608 or demo code ST001).

Medications subject to step therapy in this sample policy
Glucorin XR 500 mg tablet requires a documented trial of a preferred first-line agent from the sample formulary.

How the step is checked
The sample payer checks the patient's claims history for the prerequisite drug. If the trial was paid by a different plan or paid in cash, it may not appear in the history and documentation is needed.

Step therapy exception
The prescriber may request a step therapy exception. The request should include the history of prerequisite medications (names and dates), and the prescriber's supporting statement. Pharmacy staff can prepare the administrative parts of the request; the prescriber provides the supporting information.
