Title: Non-Formulary / Drug Not Covered Policy (Sample)

FICTIONAL SAMPLE POLICY FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. This document was written for the RxResolveAI student project. It does not describe any real insurance company, plan or medication.

Purpose
Medications that are not on the sample plan formulary are not covered. Claims for them are rejected with "Product/service not covered" or "Non-formulary drug" (reject code 70 or demo code NC001).

Non-formulary medications in this sample policy
Cardiozen 10 mg tablet is not on the MeadowCare Demo Plan formulary. Covered alternatives are listed in the sample formulary.

What staff can do
Confirm the medication and NDC were entered correctly, check the formulary for covered alternatives, and contact the prescriber about switching to a covered alternative.

Formulary exception
If the prescriber believes a covered alternative is not appropriate, a formulary exception request can be prepared. It should include the medication requested, the alternatives considered, and the prescriber's supporting statement. Authorized staff must review the request before it is submitted.
